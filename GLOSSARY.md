# SUGNL Community Website

The public website of the Sitecore User Group Netherlands: events, speakers, community blogs and the newsletter mailing list.

## Newsletter

**Newsletter**:
News sent to Subscribers from outside the website. The website only maintains who receives it; it never sends a Newsletter itself.
_Avoid_: Mailing, campaign

**Subscriber**:
A person whose email address has been confirmed for receiving the Newsletter. A Subscriber is known only by their email address.
_Avoid_: Member, contact, sign-up

**Pending Subscription**:
A request to receive the Newsletter that has not yet been confirmed by the owner of the email address. A Pending Subscription is not a Subscriber, and it expires (is erased) after 7 days without Confirmation.
_Avoid_: Unconfirmed subscriber

**Confirmation**:
The act of the email address owner proving they asked to subscribe, by following the link the website emailed them (double opt-in).
_Avoid_: Verification, activation

**Unsubscribe**:
A Subscriber ending their subscription through the personal unsubscribe link in any email the website sends them. Entering an email address on the unsubscribe page only emails that link, so nobody can unsubscribe someone else. It takes effect immediately; afterwards the website keeps no record of them.
_Avoid_: Sign off, opt out, remove

**Subscriber Export**:
The list of email addresses of all current Subscribers, taken by an Admin to send a Newsletter.
_Avoid_: Mailing list download

## People

**Organizer**:
A person who runs SUGNL and is presented publicly as such on the website.

**Admin**:
A person explicitly permitted to view and export the Subscriber list. Usually an Organizer, but not by definition; being an Organizer grants no access.
_Avoid_: Moderator, owner, organizer (when access is meant)
